Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy covers the daily rare-answer word game made by AngrySurferGames for Android (Google Play package com.angrysurfergames.rare_answers), called "the game" below.

What the game keeps

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your answers, scores, streak and past days are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them.
  • We do not run a server for the game and we do not collect anything about you ourselves.
  • COPY puts a one-line result on your clipboard, and only when you tap it.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the game may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and the rough location it implies,
  • how you interact with the ads (views and taps) and basic app activity,
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs and performance data.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. This data is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your answer at any time with the PRIVACY button on the home screen.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
