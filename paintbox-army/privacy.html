Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers Paintbox Army (颜料军团), a game made by AngrySurferGames for Android (Google Play package com.angrysurfergames.paintboxarmy) and iOS, called "the game" below.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No ads, no purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • The game plays offline and does not send anything over the internet. We do not run a server for it.
  • A few settings, such as the language and sound on/off, are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
